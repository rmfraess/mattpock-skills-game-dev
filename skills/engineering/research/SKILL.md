# Research

Delegate reading through exactly one approved, inspectable worker/session adapter when available; otherwise research directly. The worker must not redelegate. Delegation preserves the caller's context—it is not required when the runtime cannot provide a durable, visible result.

## 1. Bound the question

State the exact question, the decision it informs, in-scope versions/platforms/sources, a stopping criterion, and what the research will not establish.

## 2. Match authority to the claim

Classify each load-bearing claim before choosing sources:

- normative requirement or API contract;
- exact implementation/source behavior;
- project-specific empirical behavior;
- player-facing or creative judgment;
- source/canon fact or approved adaptation decision;
- licensing, certification, accessibility, legal, or production practice.

Use versioned official docs/specs/source for documented behavior; project configuration and executed evidence for local state; original or contractually approved material for canon; actual license/platform-holder sources for obligations. High-quality papers, technical talks, and postmortems may support practice or measured-experience claims when labeled secondary and corroborated where material. Preserve authority conflicts instead of averaging them.

For game work, apply `/game-development`. Vendor documentation cannot prove behavior in this engine fork, renderer, content set, build, or target. Route a load-bearing project behavior to the smallest `/prototype` or `/diagnosing-bugs` check and link its evidence.

## 3. Keep truth classes separate

For every material finding record **documented**, **observed**, **inferred**, or **unresolved**, plus version/revision, platform/configuration, access date, and a verifiable citation. Quote exact wording for critical or disputed facts. Never promote documentation into observed project behavior.

Record provenance, license, redistribution, and attribution constraints when recommending third-party code, plugins, assets, datasets, audio, or reference material.

## 4. Protect restricted information

Before writing, apply confidentiality and data minimization. Summarize restricted platform/IP material and store an authorized pointer; never copy secrets, NDA text, licensed source assets, or sensitive telemetry into chat or a public repository.

## 5. Write and retain intentionally

Write one cited Markdown artifact only when it is meant to become shared project context. Otherwise use the configured tracker, knowledge base, or local research area and leave a durable pointer. Follow the project's location and retention policy; date/version time-sensitive findings and name their review or expiry condition.

Done when the stopping criterion is met, every load-bearing claim has an authority and truth class, conflicts and unknowns remain visible, and the artifact names the empirical check still required before any project-specific claim can be treated as observed.
